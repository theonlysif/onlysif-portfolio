## what this is

a redesign of the vidur app, revised from the earlier proposal now that it's clear this is one progressive web app and not three. that means one responsive system across desktop and mobile rather than separate treatments, which is where most of the reduction in price comes from.

the real problem isn't any single screen - it's that there's no design system underneath, so every new feature arrives looking slightly unlike the last one. that's what this fixes.

## what i'll design

- **the main screen** - where 70-80% of the time goes. suggestions that respond to what someone actually asks about instead of sitting static, progress that says something useful during the twenty seconds a response takes, and citations that open in place instead of throwing you onto another page
- **agent workflows** - the gst appeal filing agent, designed as a pattern the next agents inherit rather than a one-off. upload, timeline, notes, output
- **the design system** - typography, colour, spacing, buttons, inputs, cards, chat and ai response components, modals, upload states, pricing blocks. things like right-click menus and alerts get designed once and used everywhere
- **the rest of the app** - document analysis, library, subscription and upgrade flows, profile, navigation, auth and onboarding

i go to full depth on each of these - every modal, every state, loading and empty and error included.

## what i'm not doing

- **no code.** figma files, component specs, and a prototype. your team builds it
- **no copy or content**
- **nothing already in your 60-day pipeline.** send me that list and i'll design around it instead of redoing work you're about to change anyway
