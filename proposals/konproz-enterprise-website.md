## what this is

konproz needs its own site - same visual family as vidur, deliberately, but standing on its own as the enterprise arm. five to six pages that say what the offer actually is, put your real numbers in front of people, and make booking a call easy. i build in code directly, so there's no figma stage before you see something real.

## what i'll design

- **home** - enterprise positioning, the hero narrative, social proof up top
- **about** - who konproz is, and why you're credible on this
- **services** - tiers laid out plainly, non-ai at inr 5l/month and ai at inr 10l/month, and what each covers
- **case studies** - built around the tangible numbers, accuracy and hours saved, with room for the short videos
- **contact** - book-a-call as the main action

desktop and mobile throughout, plus reusable blocks for case studies, testimonials, and pricing, so adding a page later doesn't mean starting over. send me baseline content and i'll write the rest around it, yours to edit whenever.
