## what this is

konproz needs to stand on its own - same visual family as vidur, deliberately, but positioned as the enterprise arm. this is the design for that site: five to six pages that make the enterprise offer legible, put real numbers in front of people, and give them an obvious way to book a call.

design only. your team builds it. the split is spelled out below so there's nothing to interpret.

## what i'll design

- **home** - enterprise positioning, hero narrative, social proof up top
- **about** - who konproz is, and why you're credible on this
- **services** - the tiers plainly laid out, non-ai at inr 5l/month and ai at inr 10l/month, with what each one covers
- **case studies** - built around tangible numbers, accuracy and hours saved, with room for the short videos
- **contact** - book-a-call as the main action

desktop and mobile for all of it, plus a small component library - case study blocks, testimonials, pricing tables - so your team can add marketing pages later without coming back to me.

you get figma files for every page, the design system and component specs, and developer-ready assets.

## what i'm not doing

- **no code.** i hand off figma and specs, your developers build it
- **no cms setup or integration**
- **no copy.** you're giving me the content
- **nothing on the existing vidur wordpress site.** moving that to react would cost you the seo you've built on a lakh-plus visitors - it should stay on wordpress, with someone who specialises in it

## timeline

- discovery and structure :: 3-4 days
- ux and wireframes :: 1 week
- high-fidelity design :: 1-1.5 weeks
- revisions and handoff :: 3-4 days
- **start to handoff** :: 3-4 weeks

## what it costs

- **design, 5-6 pages + component library** :: inr 90,000

half on kickoff, half on handoff, invoiced at each. two rounds of revisions per phase. content, brand guidelines, and the reference sites you like come to me before we start.
