reading

i save almost everything i read — articles, newsletters, books, tweets — and highlight the parts that land. this is four years of that, clustered into themes and plotted against time.

the shapes are the point. a theme that spikes once was a weekend rabbit hole. one that keeps returning is something i'm actually circling. amber marks the months i stopped to write something of my own, which turns out to be much rarer than highlighting.
