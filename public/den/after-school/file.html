on the night of 11 october 2016 i stopped editing and sent you the whole thing. this is that file, exactly as it went.

for her

I have been absolutely and completely in love with you since November. And I really can't think of a better way to put this, but, I love you. As everything. You have no idea how happy you make me. People told me you were dating someone, but that wouldn't stop me from telling you what I want, but then I thought things were really good where they were. I don't know. I really really love you. Just you. I can’t be with someone else, I just can’t. And you're the best I'll ever talk to. And I want to be able to talk to you even 40 years from now. I can't watch you live life without me being a part. I love you, Neshwa. I really do.

this is what i had actually planned instead of all this. before i knew you'd leave to india and all that.

the entries

17 april 2016

My mom isn't fine. And I just got back from the hospital. And the only thing in my mind is how the fuck do I not tell you how much I love you and fuck things up. It's hard. Very hard. I love you.

25 may 2016

You’ve been grounded from 15th May, and I miss you, a lot. Wish we could catch up without even texting. So, you came on today for a while. But couldn’t talk much. You had so little time and I was busy. But I’m happy you’re okay. I missed you really bad. I can wait longer. A lot more longer. I love you.

8 july 2016

Okay, so India sucks. For some reason I hate it when you’re in India. I hate not knowing how you are. And I hate not texting. So, yeah, it sucks. Really hoping you’re doing good and fine. This is satisfying.

8 september 2016

Only now have i realized I’m writing this exactly two months after the last one, complete coincidence I swear. Well, recently we haven't been talking much. I’m guessing it’s your internet, I’m pretty sure it is. But, still. I miss you. A lot. I miss talking to you, you were a pleasure. And despite your absence, I am completely in love with you, Neshwa, I can’t stop loving you. But I can wait for you. Although my odds are high. I am sure it’s worth everything.
